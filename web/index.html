How to play

  • On your turn, click a face-down piece to flip it, OR move one of your own face-up pieces.
  • Each piece captures any adjacent enemy of equal or lower rank.
  • Soldier can capture General (1 beats 7). General cannot capture Soldier.
  • Cannon jumps along a row or column over exactly one piece to capture anything, ignoring rank.
  • The first flipper plays the revealed color. You lose if you have no legal move.
