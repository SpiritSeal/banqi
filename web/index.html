Capture rules

        
    
          
  • Default: a piece captures any adjacent enemy of equal or lower rank.
    • 
          
  • Soldier 兵/卒 captures General 帥/將 (rank 1 beats rank 7).
    • 
          
  • General cannot capture Soldier.
    • 
          
  • Cannon 炮/砲 never captures adjacently. It jumps along a row or column over exactly one screen piece (any color, face-up or face-down) and lands on the target. Cannon ignores rank and may capture face-down pieces — they're revealed on capture.
    • 
          
  • You may flip any face-down piece on your turn instead of moving. The very first flip determines your color: the first flipper plays the revealed color.
    • 
          
  • Win: opponent has no legal move (no flips, no movable pieces) or no remaining pieces.
